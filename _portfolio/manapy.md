## Manapy: Parallel Finite Volume Solver for Solving Fluid Flow Problems on Complex Geometries

**GitHub:** [https://github.com/imadki/manapy](https://github.com/imadki/manapy)

**Role:** Initiator and main developer

**Description:**
Manapy is an open-source Python library for parallel resolution of partial differential equations (PDEs) using the finite volume method on unstructured meshes. It supports a wide range of physical models including:

- Shallow water equations
- Navier-Stokes equations
- Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD)
- Viscoplastic flows
- Flow in fibrous media

The library is built on top of MPI4py for distributed memory parallelism, allowing simulations on clusters and supercomputers.

**Current Users:**
Master's and PhD students, as well as researchers and faculty members from UM6P, USPN, ULHN, Groupe de Recherche en Génie Electrique de Paris, and CentraleSupélec.
