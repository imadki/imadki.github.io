This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the C programming language covering the history of the language, syntax, variables, functions, strings, and structures. A key focus is on structures and their benefits as contiguous data. Students develop a deep understanding of the differences between arrays and pointers, including dynamic allocation and deallocation, and the performance impact of using heap versus stack memory.
