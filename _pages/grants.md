## Active Grants

---

**Coupling Physical Models and Data for Monitoring Soil Evolution**
- **Role:** Principal Investigator
- **Funding:** Nutricrops – Office Chérifien des Phosphates (OCP), Morocco
- **Budget:** €375K
- **Period:** September 2025 – 2027 (2 years)
- **Summary:** Development of a model for the assessment and improvement of agricultural soil health, combining physical modeling, HPC numerical simulation, and big data (sensors, satellites, field campaigns). Objectives include characterizing soil evolution, developing soil health indicators via machine learning, and providing decision-support tools for sustainable agricultural practices.

---

**PINNs-Driven Optimization and Modeling in Catalytic Graphitization Processes**
- **Role:** Principal Investigator
- **Funding:** Materials Science & Nanoengineering (MSN) – UM6P
- **Budget:** €36K
- **Period:** March 2025 – March 2026 (12 months)
- **Summary:** Development of a hybrid modeling framework for catalytic graphitization processes by coupling experimental data, physical equations, and process simulation. The project integrates Aspen-based simulations with machine learning to build neural-network surrogate models for process optimization and scale-up.

---

**Deep Multimodal Learning and Continuous Learning for Fault Prognosis and Real-time Supervision of the Moroccan TRIGA Mark II Reactor**
- **Role:** Principal Investigator
- **Funding:** UM6P – Centre National de l'Énergie, des Sciences et des Techniques Nucléaires (CNESTEN), Morocco
- **Budget:** €176K
- **Period:** June 2024 – 2028 (4 years)
- **Summary:** Development of an innovative platform for supervision and predictive maintenance of the TRIGA Mark II reactor using hybrid PINNs for early anomaly detection, continuous learning for adaptation to operating conditions, and improved operational safety through predictive maintenance.

---

**Combined Use of Numerical Modelling and Sediment Radiometric Dating for Environmental Reconstruction and Management of the Marchica Lagoon**
- **Role:** Co-Investigator (Project Leader: Otman Abida)
- **Funding:** UM6P – CNESTEN, Morocco
- **Budget:** €145K
- **Period:** June 2024 – 2028 (4 years)
- **Summary:** Analysis of heavy metal pollution in the Marchica Lagoon (north Morocco) combining hydrodynamic modeling, field sampling, sediment coring, and radiometric dating (Pb-210, Cs-137) to predict pollutant evolution and propose sustainable management measures.

---

**Multiphysics and HPC Chair**
- **Role:** Principal Investigator (co-PI: Prof. Abdelouahed El Fatimi, IAP)
- **Funding:** OCP Group, Morocco
- **Budget:** €6.3M (50% College of Computing, 50% Institute of Applied Physics)
- **Period:** October 2022 – 2027 (5 years)
- **Summary:** Research chair aimed at developing numerical platforms for large-scale multiphysics modeling and simulation, structured around four axes: complex fluids and fluid-structure interaction; computational mechanics of composite materials; HPC for multiphysics simulation and AI; and supersonic and multiphase flows.
