Teaching Statement

By learning you will teach; by teaching you will learn

As a teacher with a dual background in computer science and mathematics, I have taught subjects ranging from shell scripting and Python to C/C++ programming, algorithms and data structures, object-oriented programming, high-performance computing, calculus, and numerical methods. I favor a learning by doing approach: students work on real projects from the start of the semester, with theory and interactive problem-solving sessions on platforms such as Codeforces reinforcing the concepts needed. My teaching evaluations consistently average between 4.0 and 4.8 out of 5.
